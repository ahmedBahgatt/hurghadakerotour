## Cannibalization guardrails

- **Quad Safari vs Super Safari:** quad-only, village + evening vs the full
  program adding jeep 4x4 + spider car. Titles/descriptions/keywords differentiate
  "safari quad" vs "super safari / jeep 4x4 / spider car".
- **Three Islands vs Orange Bay + Hula Hula:** three named stops (Orange Bay +
  Magawish + White Island) vs two (Orange Bay + Hula Hula). Distinct galleries,
  distinct primary keyword.
- **Dolphin House vs Private Speedboat:** shared-group wild-dolphin + reef snorkel
  trip vs a private-boat charter (per-boat price). Different intent (activity vs
  private rental).
- **Cairo/GEM vs Luxor:** different cities/monuments; "Le Caire / Kair / Cairo"
  vs "Louxor / Luksor / Luxor", each with its own landmark long-tails.

## Notes on language, not geography

French-language pages are **not** France-only (French-speaking tourists come from
Belgium, Switzerland, Canada, the Maghreb, etc.); likewise PL/RO. hreflang targets
**language**, not country - no country-specific `fr-FR`/`ro-RO` region codes are
used. This is intentional (see `SEO_AUDIT.md` checkpoint 15).
